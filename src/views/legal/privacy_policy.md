**Last updated: October 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains what StudentHive collects, why, who can see it, and the choices you have. We follow the principles of the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173): we collect only what the app needs, we use it only for the purposes below, and we keep it secure.

### 1. What we collect
- **Account details:** your full name, school email address and password. Your password is handled by our sign-in provider and is stored only in scrambled (hashed) form. We cannot read it.
- **Profile details you choose to add:** course or major, short bio, skills, portfolio and social media links, and a profile photo.
- **Listings and activity:** what you post (titles, descriptions, rates, photos), booking requests and their dates, prices and status, and reviews and ratings you give or receive.
- **Sign-in cookie:** a small cookie named `sh_refresh` keeps you signed in for up to 30 days on your browser. Logging out removes it.

We do **not** collect payment card or bank details, because StudentHive does not process payments. We do not ask for your student ID.

### 2. Why we use it
- To create your account and verify that you are a student.
- To show your profile, listings and reviews to other students so you can find and book each other.
- To run the app: bookings, notifications on screen, calendar, ratings and security.
- To keep the community safe, for example to remove abusive content.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

### 3. Who can see it
- Other **signed-in students** can see your name, course, bio, skills, links, photo, active listings and reviews. Anyone not signed in cannot browse profiles.
- Your **email address** is used to sign in and to identify you in the app. Take care with what you put in your bio and links, because other students can read it.
- The people you book with can see the details of that booking.

### 4. Where it is stored
StudentHive uses **Supabase** (database, sign-in and file storage) and a web host to run the app. They process data on our behalf to provide the service. Profile and listing photos are stored in public file storage so they can be shown in the app, which means anyone with a photo's exact link can open it.

### 5. How long we keep it
We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account from the Profile page, your profile, listings, gigs and rentals, and reviews are permanently deleted. Backups held by our providers may take a short time to clear.

### 6. Your rights
Under the Data Privacy Act you may ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to how it is used, and to complain to the National Privacy Commission. You can edit most of your information on the Profile page and delete your account there. For anything else, contact us at **{contact}**.

### 7. Security
We use sign-in provider security, row-level access rules in the database, and encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure, so please choose a strong, unique password and do not share it. If you notice something wrong with your account, tell us straight away.

### 8. Children
StudentHive is meant for college students. It is not intended for children.

### 9. Changes
We may update this policy. If we make an important change, we will update the date above.
